Who we are

Countr is a service of Exter Group Private Limited ("Exter", "we", "us"), a company registered in India with its registered office at GF VINAYAKARESIDENCY, SAI NIRAJAN COLONY, Chinamushidiwada, Visakhapatnam, Visakhapatnam - 531173, Andhra Pradesh. Countr lets restaurants take food orders on their own WhatsApp Business number. An automated assistant replies to customers on the restaurant's behalf, takes the order, arranges payment and delivery, and sends order updates. The restaurant manages its orders in the Countr dashboard.

This policy covers Countr only. The rest of the exter-ai.com website is covered by our website privacy policy.

Who this policy covers

  • Customers: people who message a restaurant that uses Countr, on WhatsApp or through a Countr order page.
  • Restaurants: the businesses that use Countr, and their owners and staff who use the Countr dashboard.

When you order from a restaurant, the restaurant decides why your data is collected, and we process it on the restaurant's behalf to run the service. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the restaurant is the Data Fiduciary for its customers' data and Exter is its Data Processor. Each restaurant also publishes its own policies on its Countr page at exter-ai.com/r/<restaurant>.

Information we collect

From customers

  • WhatsApp details: your phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • Messages: the text, voice notes, images, button taps and list choices you send to the restaurant, and the replies you receive.
  • Order details: the items, quantities, notes (for example "less spicy" or an allergy), prices, delivery charge, order history, favourite dishes and any loyalty points or coupons.
  • Delivery details: your delivery address and, if you share it, your location pin.
  • Payment status: whether you paid, how much, the method, and the payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on the payment provider's page and never reach us.
  • Feedback: ratings and comments you give after an order, and whether you have asked not to receive offers.

From restaurants

  • Business details: name, address, location, GSTIN, FSSAI licence, opening hours, menu, prices and dish photos.
  • Owner and staff details: names, phone numbers for order alerts, and dashboard login details. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Connection details for the restaurant's WhatsApp Business account, payment gateway and delivery provider. Access tokens and secret keys are stored encrypted.

Collected automatically

Technical logs such as message delivery status, error reports and timestamps, used to keep the service running. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers in Countr.

How we use it

We use personal data only to provide Countr to restaurants and their customers:

  • to understand your messages and reply, show the menu, take and confirm your order, and answer questions about it;
  • to calculate delivery charges, check the restaurant can deliver to you, and book a delivery partner;
  • to create payment links and process refunds;
  • to send you updates about your order (accepted, on the way, delivered);
  • to remember your saved addresses and usual orders so reordering is quicker;
  • to show the restaurant its orders and chats, and let its staff take over a conversation at any time;
  • to send occasional offers from the restaurant on WhatsApp, only where WhatsApp's rules allow it. Reply STOP at any time to stop them;
  • to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems, and meet our legal and tax obligations.

Replies are written by an automated AI assistant. To do this, the content of your conversation is sent to the AI model providers listed below. They process it only to generate replies for us.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, build advertising profiles, or share one restaurant's customers with another restaurant.

Data from Meta and WhatsApp

Countr connects to a restaurant's WhatsApp Business account only when the restaurant authorises it, through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Data we receive from Meta (messages, phone numbers, profile names, message status and catalog data) is used only to provide Countr to that restaurant as described in this policy, in line with the Meta Platform Terms and the WhatsApp Business policies. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or combine it with data from other sources to profile people. A restaurant can disconnect Countr from its WhatsApp account at any time.

How long we keep it

  • Conversations, order history and saved addresses: for as long as the restaurant uses Countr, so the service can work and you can reorder. We delete them sooner if you ask (see how to delete your data).
  • Order and payment records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, even after a deletion request. These are kept separate from your chats and used only for that purpose.
  • Restaurant data: for as long as the restaurant uses Countr. When a restaurant leaves, we delete its data within 90 days, except records the law requires us to keep.
  • Technical logs: kept for a short time for troubleshooting, with personal details removed where possible.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit. Restaurant access tokens and secret keys are encrypted in storage, and passwords are stored only as hashes. Each restaurant's data is kept separate from every other restaurant's, and access is limited to people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell the affected restaurants and, where the law requires it, the authorities and you.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop offers: reply STOP in the chat. You will still receive updates about orders you place.
  • Talk to a person: ask the assistant for a person at any time, and restaurant staff will reply.
  • Access or correct your data: ask us or the restaurant for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected.
  • Delete your data: see below.
  • Withdraw consent or complain: contact us. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete the data Countr holds about you in any of these ways:

  • Email support@exter-ai.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  • Send a WhatsApp message to +91 72001 41512.
  • Use the form on our support page.

Tell us the WhatsApp number you used and, if you know them, the restaurants you ordered from. We may message that number to confirm the request came from you. Within 30 days we delete your messages, saved addresses, favourites and profile from Countr, and confirm when it is done. Order and payment records that Indian law requires us to keep are kept only for that purpose and deleted when the retention period ends.

Restaurants can ask us to delete all their data by writing to the same address from their registered email or phone number.

Children

Countr is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used Countr, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or the law changes. We will change the date at the top, and tell restaurants in advance of any significant change.
