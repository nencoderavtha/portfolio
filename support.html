Urgent during service? WhatsApp or call us rather than emailing, and start with your restaurant name.

For restaurant owners

Contact us about any of these:

TopicWhat to include
Orders & the botThe customer's number and order number, and what the bot said or did.
Deliveries & ridersThe order number and what went wrong (no rider, late pickup, wrong address).
Payments & refundsThe order number and, for online payments, the Razorpay payment ID.
Menu, prices & timingsThe dish names and the change you want. You can edit most of these yourself in the dashboard.
Dashboard loginYour restaurant name and login username. Never send us your password.

For customers

If you ordered food through a restaurant's WhatsApp, the restaurant handles your order, delivery and refunds. The fastest route is to message the restaurant in the same WhatsApp chat with your order number. Each restaurant's contact details and its cancellation, refund and delivery policies are on its own page at exter-ai.com/r/<restaurant>.

If you can't reach the restaurant, or the problem is with the ordering service itself, contact us and we'll follow it up with them.

How we handle your data, and how to have it deleted, is set out in the Countr Privacy Policy.
